Everything you need to know about talking to strangers safely on Omegooo — the world's premier free random video chat alternative.

What is Omegooo and how is it different from Omegle? ▼
Omegooo is a modern, 100% free random video chat platform designed as a safe, high-speed successor to Omegle. Unlike legacy platforms, Omegooo features real-time AI computer vision moderation (NSFWJS), instant interest-based matching, encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer streaming, and zero requirements for registration, apps, or credit cards.
Is Omegooo completely free and anonymous? ▼
Yes! You are completely anonymous. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number, or passwords. Your video stream connects directly to your partner through encrypted P2P channels and is never saved or stored on any server.
How does Interest Matching work? ▼
Before clicking "Video chat", simply type your favorite hobbies or topics (e.g., gaming, music, anime, programming, travel) into the interest box and press Enter. Omegooo will prioritize matching you with strangers around the world who share those exact tags.
How does Omegooo prevent inappropriate content? ▼
We prioritize community safety above all else. Omegooo uses automated real-time AI visual analysis to scan for prohibited or explicit content continuously. Additionally, our 1-click user report system allows instant flagging of bad actors, permanently restricting their access via automated IP and device fingerprint banning.
Do I need to install any app or plugin? ▼
No downloads or plugins are required. Omegooo runs natively in all modern web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows with full mobile touch and desktop camera optimization.
